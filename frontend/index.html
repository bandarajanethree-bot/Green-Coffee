Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Exceptional Coffee

Green Coffee Café began in 2016 with a simple belief: that coffee should be more than a habit — it should be an experience. Nestled in the heart of Colombo, we source directly from mountain estates across Sri Lanka, Ethiopia, and Colombia.

Every bean is hand-selected, every roast calibrated to perfection. We're not just a café — we're a community of people who believe that great coffee can change your day.

Who We Are

Our Story

A love letter to coffee, community, and our beautiful island.

From a Tiny Roastery to a Community Hub

It started in 2016 with a roasting drum, a leased garage, and an obsession with the perfect cup. Founders Kavya and Dinesh left their corporate careers to pursue what they truly loved — connecting people through exceptional coffee.

Today, Green Coffee Café spans two locations in Colombo and Kandy, with direct relationships with 15 farming families across Sri Lanka and East Africa.
